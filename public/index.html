LEARN THE HEIST

Simple rules.
Interesting choices.

01

Draw, then play.

Draw one card, then capture or discard a card from your hand. Discarding passes the turn.

02

Match the rank.

A 7 matches any other 7. Capture all matching table cards together. Suits don’t affect matching.

03

Steal the top.

Match a rival’s top card to steal its consecutive matching group. The rest of their pile stays put. You can steal from several rivals at once.

04

Keep it going.

You may also match your own top card. Every capture earns another draw while the deck has cards. Once it’s empty, play without drawing and pass after every move.

2—10 5 pointsJ · Q · K 10 pointsA 20 points

When all hands and the deck are empty, the highest captured-pile score wins. Ties share the win. Cards left on the table score nothing.

Keyboard: use ← → to select a card, Enter to capture, D to draw, X to discard, and Escape to clear the selection.
